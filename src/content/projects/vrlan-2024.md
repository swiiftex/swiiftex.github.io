The shutdown of Echo VR forced us to change the direction of this event. It shattered the competitive VR space we knew and loved, and split the players — and therefore our attendees — across many more games.

This event became more of a showcase, with A2 at its centre: Another Axiom's spiritual successor to Echo VR, which has since become **Orion Drift**. We worked with Another Axiom to provide the first public broadcast of their game in alpha, which did put a fair bit of pressure on me to get it right. The streams went out under the VRMEET 2024 banner, from The Lemon Grove at the University of Exeter.

I worked with software developer notblue to create bespoke software for the event, as I needed an overlay that worked in multiple games — A2, Breachers, Racket Club, Blaston and Onward.
