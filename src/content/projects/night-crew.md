> A lo-fi mall with high-key problems. Foul creatures roam the halls — and management still expects YOU to mop the floor. Even though the floor ate Gary. Welcome to the Night Crew. You are *definitely* not paid enough for this.

Night Crew is a co-op horror game from **Binary Spiders**, published under their Upstart Games label. It launched on Steam in Early Access on 14 November 2026.

You and up to five friends work the night shift at Twilight Palms Mall, an 80s shopping centre that gets stranger every night. You fix cabling, clean up trash and finish whatever else management wants done, while hiding from the creatures that roam the halls. The money you earn goes on paying off your debt or on items that make the next night easier.

The team plans to release the first big update, with new monsters, new areas of the mall, new items and new cosmetics, within a few weeks of launch. Modding support is planned too.
