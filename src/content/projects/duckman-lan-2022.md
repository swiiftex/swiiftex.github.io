In April 2022 I got to produce and present a professional broadcast package for an in-person esports event in Bournemouth — the first community-run LAN in Europe for Ready At Dawn's **Echo Arena**, with mixed and pro team days.

I worked with Annexed Fate to produce the livestream package for the event, which had multiple sponsors and was received really well by the community. I used OBS, UPDeck, Photoshop and After Effects, along with my own [Broadcast Tool](/broadcast-tool/), to pull it off. I also worked with Blackmagic cameras and interfaces, GoPros and a PA system to give both the livestream and the in-person audience a great viewing experience.

For the passion of the game, and for the community.
