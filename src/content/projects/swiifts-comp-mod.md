Lots of changes to MORDHAU to better suit competitive play. New to the terminology? Try my [melee slashers quick start guide](/melee-slashers/).

## Mechanic changes

- Chambers disabled
- Jump kick removed
- Kick feint removed
- Kicks no longer flinch release
- Consecutive feints (within 1350ms of each other) cost an extra 5 stamina
- Health regen disabled in SKM
- Combo after miss disabled

## Value changes

- All parries are now 325ms (kite shield, round shield etc. brought down to the default)
- Stab feint window nerfed to 70% of windup
- All strike feint windows nerfed to 92.5% of windup
- Lower strike angle greatly reduced
- Weapon release capped at 475ms — weapons with a faster release are unchanged

## Banned perks and items

- Tank
- Flesh Wound
- Healing items

## Maps

- FFA_Moshpit_Comp and SKM_Moshpit_Comp
- FFA_Yard_Comp and SKM_Yard_Comp
